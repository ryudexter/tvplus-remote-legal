# TVPlus Remote

A Wi-Fi remote for TV+ boxes and TVs, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It needs iOS 18, iPadOS 18 or watchOS 10 or later.

This page is TVPlus Remote's support page. The app's code isn't here.

TVPlus Remote is an independent app. It isn't made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Turkcell or TV+. TV+ is a trademark of Turkcell, used here only to say which service the app opens.

## What it works with

- **TV+ boxes:** TV+ Ready (Android TV) and TV+ Pro (Google TV). In the app, tap Add a TV › TV+, or let it find the box on your Wi-Fi, then type the 6-character code the TV shows.
- **TVs with the TV+ app** that the app can open it on: Google TV and Android TV, Fire TV, LG webOS and NetCast, Samsung, Roku, Panasonic, Sony and Apple TV.
- **The remote itself** (keys, volume, channel numbers, typing) also works on Philips, Vestel, TCL and Sharp TVs, which can't open TV+ from the app yet.

Your iPhone or iPad and the TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

## Getting help

Email **[zcengel@yahoo.com](mailto:zcengel@yahoo.com)**, or in the app tap Settings › Support. Say which box or TV you have (brand and model) and what happens.

## Common questions

**The app doesn't find my box or TV.** The TV needs to be on, on the same Wi-Fi. When iOS asks whether TVPlus Remote can find devices on your local network, tap Allow. If you said no earlier, turn it on in the iPhone's or iPad's Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. You can also add it by its IP address: Settings › Devices › Add a TV by IP address.

**How do I type a channel number?** Turn the switch in the pad's corner to **123** and type the number; the TV changes channel. The 123 key at the top opens the number pad too.

**What does the TV+ button do?** It opens the TV+ app on your box or TV. It's dimmed on TVs that can't open apps from a phone.

**What does Premium+ include?** Several saved TVs (without it, the app keeps one), the Apple Watch app after its 2 free weeks, Gyro Pointer, Siri, the remote widget and the TV Button in Control Center. It's a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription with a free trial. Family Sharing works with both.

**I bought Premium+ but it isn't showing.** In Settings, tap Restore Purchases below Premium+, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I cancel the subscription?** On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in the app tap Settings › Premium+. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
